Data: Chicago Traffic Crashes, City of Chicago open data portal, cleaned and clustered per community area with haversine DBSCAN (eps 250 m, min 12 crashes). Severity weights: 3 fatal, 2 incapacitating, 1 non-incapacitating.

Gi* is computed in esri_pipeline.py in NumPy with a 3 km fixed distance band. Several of the 95%+ hot spots (O'Hare, Hegewisch) hold fewer than 100 crashes, so their severity rates are noisy. Drive-time polygons come from the ArcGIS Network Analyst service-area service (ArcGIS Location Platform). Rebuild with python -m GeospatialRisk.Chicago.build_live_map.
